# v3.1: Temporal and Unary Canonicalization

The November 21, 2026 experiment used commit `6648d7bd4148e6374a5446eaf12a3f1d7633db52` on the original Alloy4Fun corpus. The paired experiment and the full-reference search answer different questions. Their zero pairs must **not** be added.

**Student versus oracle.** Of 66,080 original files, all 61,598 distinct-AST pairs had matching native operations at the frozen normalized-IR boundary: zero unchecked, invalid, discrepant or error outcomes. Among 19,212 correct pairs, canonical distance was zero for **4,413**, up from 4,174 in v3, with no previous zero lost. None of the 42,386 incorrect pairs was merged. The other 4,482 files had identical raw ASTs; a separate run checked their selected predicates in both directions. They were not discarded as unsupported.

**Search against all correct references.** The Augmenter ranked all 42,386 incorrect predicates in their respective invariant groups, making **1,894,168 recorded comparisons**; another 21 raw-AST-identical cross-file comparisons are accounted separately, all had different reported support contexts and were not measured. There were no recorded distance discrepancies or incorrect nearest-zero matches among the measured AST-distinct comparisons. Mean nearest distance was **12.267140** for raw ASTs and **11.505356** for canonical forms. Within the correct pools, 4,496 distinct AST representatives formed **2,028** connected components under recorded zero distance. The **11,882** unordered distinct-AST correct-pool zero pairs preserve all 11,607 from v3 and add 275. Identities, source bodies, and prior raw distances were checked, not just totals.

**Why coverage improved.** For unary relations, domain and range restriction are intersection; restricting a higher-arity relation is a different operation. Universal quantification commutes with `always` over a static domain: every atom in the same set must satisfy the body at every future instant. Movement across a changing domain is unsound. The source rules also distinguish a value captured at the binding instant from the carrier observed in the next state, and restrict a quantified domain only with the required subset evidence. See the [Lean source laws](../../section3-repair-audit/formal/Phase5SourceRules.lean) and [Java scheduling controls](../../../src/is/fivefivefive/CanDis/CapabilitySourceSchedulingTest.java).

**Controls.** Both paths agreed on all 98 focus pairs: seven newly zero and 91 nonzero. All nine satisfiable `trainStationOld/inv10` counterexamples remained distinct. The additional `trash_ltl/inv17` family supplied 24 equivalent pairs, checked in both native directions. The 1,232- and 4,072-pair regressions matched in all 2,464 and 8,144 directions. All 239 new paired zeros had satisfiable facts and no counterexample to equivalence of the selected predicates under the original Alloy bounds. The 4,721-file supplement (4,482 identical + 239 gains) recorded 9,442 matching directions, rejected all 956 prescribed corruptions, and took at most 2.409 seconds per file. An earlier supplement stopped in accounting on a wrong manifest path; its outputs were not counted. The entire supplement was repeated under the corrected auditor.

Agreement of recorded cross-reference distances is not an exhaustive cross-reference action replay; not every nonzero correct-pool comparison is serialized.
