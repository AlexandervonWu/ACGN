\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Typed flexible-arity slotted e-graphs unify algebraic container quotients
with typed variable interfaces by separating an expression's effective-support
kernel from its caller placement. Over the abstract formal model, we proved that
complete finite quotient presentations yield exact canonical shapes,
structural realization and compatible shape collisions preserve source
equations, and updates satisfying a local coherence invariant give sound
judgments between finite unfoldings.

On the Alloy4Fun corpus both rewrite paths retained every recorded structural
zero, including those found by original Slotted, and added 2,909 correct
student--oracle pairs beyond Slotted. Neither path assigned zero to an
incorrect submission. Command-specific rules recognized 259 further
correct pairs. On full-reference search the paths agreed on all 1,894,168
scalar scores. Native replay also agreed on the checked reference subsets.
The historical synthetic grid shows how heterogeneous slot types can reduce
the cost of searching binder renamings.
