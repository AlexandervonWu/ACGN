\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Typed flexible-arity slotted e-graphs unify algebraic container quotients
with typed variable interfaces by separating an expression's effective-support
kernel from its caller placement. Over the abstract formal model, we proved that
complete finite quotient presentations yield exact canonical shapes,
structural realization and compatible shape collisions preserve source
equations, and updates satisfying a local coherence invariant give sound
judgments between finite unfoldings.

In the \texttt{v3.3} Alloy study, both rewrite paths retained all 2,228
original slotted zeros and recognized 2,271 more correct pairs, without
an incorrect zero. Comparing under the original command recognized 259
further correct pairs using fixed-command rules. Nearest-reference search
over 1,894,168 comparisons yielded mean distances of 12.267
for the raw AST and 11.477 for the canonical projection. Certificate
integration adds overhead for typed transitions, graph rebuilding and
finite unfolding. The historical synthetic study isolates a complementary
effect. Typed slot partitions reduce the renaming search when binders have
different types. The scoped result remains tied to its command bounds.
